Privacy Policy

This page is maintained by the Zapr team to answer common privacy questions about the app.

In short

Zapr is a client-side converter. Your files are processed entirely inside your browser. We do not upload, store, or transmit your documents or images to any server.

Data we collect

The app itself does not collect personal information through forms, accounts, or analytics integrations. The hosting platform that serves Zapr may receive standard request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) for the purpose of delivering the page.

Files you convert

Conversions (Images ↔ PDF, PDF ↔ DOCX, image format changes) run locally using JavaScript libraries loaded into your browser. The file contents never leave your device.

Cookies & tracking

Zapr does not set tracking cookies or use third-party analytics in this version of the app.

How to use Zapr

A quick guide to convert your files safely, right in your browser.

1. Pick a tool

Choose the conversion you need from the toolbar — PDF, Images, Word, or Excel. Click the tool card to select it.

2. Drop your file(s)

Drag and drop your file onto the drop zone, or click to browse. Some tools accept multiple files (like Images → PDF).

3. Adjust settings (optional)

For Word ↔ Excel conversions, you can choose heading levels and whether to include paragraph text as a Content sheet.

4. Preview & download

Hit Convert and preview the result. If it looks good, download it. Batch conversions let you preview each file before grabbing the ZIP.

5. Everything stays private

All conversions run locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

Need help?

If something is not working, visit the Contact page to report a bug.
